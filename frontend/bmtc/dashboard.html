🧭 International Visitor & Tourist Transit Guide

  • Payment methods on the bus: You can pay the conductor directly via Indian UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe) by scanning the conductor's dynamic machine QR, or in cash (carry small ₹10, ₹20, or ₹50 notes).
  • Unlimited Day Pass: Taking 3 or more trips today? Ask the conductor for a Daily Transit Pass: ₹70 for unlimited Ordinary buses, or ₹120 (Vajra Gold Pass) for unlimited travel on both Ordinary & AC Volvo buses all day.
  • Airport Transit: Vayu Vajra luxury Volvo buses operate 24/7 directly between Kempegowda International Airport (KIA) and major city centers.
